## Lesson 10: Team-Based Learning (TBL) & `Tidyverse` Challenge

| Time | Activity |
| ------ | ----------- |
| 20 mins | Team-Based Worksheet |

# Part I: Team-Based Worksheet

**Paper to be discussed for today's class is Himes.et.al.2014**

You will have 20 minutes during class to work through the TBL worksheet. 

# Part II: Paper Discussion

After completing the first portion of the worksheet, we will discuss the paper as a class.

The discussion will focus on connecting the experimental design, RNA-seq results, and biological conclusions. During this portion of class, students will discuss a figure, table, or analysis.

# Part III: `Tidyverse` Challenge Worksheet 

For the final portion of today's class, you will apply the `tidyverse` skills we have been learning to the penguins dataset.

You will create your own R Markdown document and complete a series of short coding challenges. 

**Your Goal**

Complete all five challenges and make sure your code runs without errors. You will have 30 minutes in class to begin the activity. If needed, you may continue working on the activity after class.
