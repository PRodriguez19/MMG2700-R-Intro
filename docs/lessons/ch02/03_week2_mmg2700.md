## Team-Based Learning (TBL) (~40 mins)

**What is your job during TBL?**

Throughout the semester, we will use Team-Based Learning (TBL) activities to practice the skills scientists use when reading research papers, analyzing data, and communicating scientific results. You will need to come to class prepared to discuss the review article and use it to answer questions about RNA-seq data analysis.

For this first session, we will review the article together called, *"From bench to bytes: a practical guide to RNA sequencing data analysis"* by Dawadi et al. 2025. 


### Before Class: Individual Pre-Work

**Each TBL session will include preparation that must be completed before class.**

Your preparation will include:

+ Reading and interpreting a scientific paper or review article.
+ Examining a scientific figure or visualization.
+ Answering questions about an analysis, figure, or dataset.

You are not expected to have the perfect answer before class. You are expected to have an answer and be prepared to explain your reasoning.

For this first session, we will review the article together called, *"From bench to bytes: a practical guide to RNA sequencing data analysis"* by Dawadi et al. 2025. 

+ Students will be given a list of questions to answer during class with their assigned groups. 
+ We will then review the answers together as a class based on the review article. 

### TBL Goals
By the end of the TBL activities, you should be able to:

+ Interpret scientific figures and data rather than simply describe them.
+ Evaluate evidence and distinguish between what the data show and what you infer from them.
+ Consider alternative explanations for scientific observations.
+ Communicate scientific results using appropriate terminology and visual evidence.
+ Defend a scientific conclusion using evidence and clear reasoning.
