# Team-Based Learning (TBL) (~30 mins)

## What is Team-Based Learning?

Throughout the semester, some class sessions will use **Team-Based Learning (TBL)** to practice the skills scientists use when reading research papers, interpreting data, and communicating scientific findings.

TBL is a structured, team-based approach to learning. You will first prepare **individually before class**, then work with your classmates to apply what you learned to questions, figures, and scientific problems. The goal is not simply to find the "right" answer. You should be able to **explain your reasoning, evaluate evidence, and discuss your interpretation with your team**.

### What is your job during TBL?

Your job is to **come to class prepared to participate**. You do not need to know everything or have a perfect answer. You do need to have completed the assigned preparation and be ready to explain how you arrived at your answer.

For this first TBL session, we will use the review article *From bench to bytes: a practical guide to RNA sequencing data analysis* by Dawadi et al. (2025).

## Before Class: Individual Pre-Work

**Each TBL session will include preparation that must be completed before class.**

Your preparation may include:

* Reading and interpreting a scientific paper or review article.
* Examining and explaining a scientific figure or visualization.
* Answering questions about an analysis, figure, or dataset.
* Preparing to explain and defend your reasoning during the class discussion.

### TBL Preparation Checklist

Before coming to class, make sure you have completed each of the following:

- [x] Read the assigned article or the assigned sections of the article.
- [x] Identify the main question or purpose of the article.
- [x] Identify at least one concept, method, or analysis that is important for understanding RNA-seq data.
- [x] Examine the assigned figure(s) or visualization(s).
- [x] Write down your interpretation of what the figure is showing.
- [ ] Complete the assigned questions or analysis (during class).

## TBL Goals
By the end of the TBL activities, you should be able to:

+ Interpret scientific figures and data rather than simply describe them.
+ Evaluate evidence and distinguish between what the data show and what you infer from them.
+ Consider alternative explanations for scientific observations.
+ Communicate scientific results using appropriate terminology and visual evidence.
+ Defend a scientific conclusion using evidence and clear reasoning.
