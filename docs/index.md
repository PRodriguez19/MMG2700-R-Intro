# MMG2700: Foundations in R for Life Sciences

## Introduction 

This course introduces students to programming in R, with a focus on applications in microbial and cancer genomics. Through a combination of lectures and labs, students will learn R syntax, data wrangling with the tidyverse, exploratory statistics, and data visualization with ggplot2. In the second half of the course, students will explore discipline-specific topics, including antimicrobial resistance (AMR) gene detection, microbiome composition analysis, and the exploration of transcriptional and mutational profiles in cancer datasets. By the end of the course, students will be equipped with computational skills that support both modern microbial research and clinical cancer applications.
